## Emailing companies

Read the rules in [trial-campaign.md](trial-campaign.md) first. In short: limited companies and LLPs can
be emailed without prior consent if we say who we are and offer a way out; treat sole traders and anyone
unsure as individuals, who generally need to have agreed first.

### Building the list

1. Search for short-let and serviced accommodation management companies, town by town.
2. Check Companies House that each is a limited company or LLP, and note the number.
3. Take only the contact address the company publishes for enquiries.
4. Keep the list in a spreadsheet **outside this project folder**, with these columns:
   `Company, Company number, Town, Website, Contact address, Where found, Properties (if stated),
   First emailed, Followed up, Reply, Opted out`
5. Mark every opt-out and never email them again.

Send 10 to 15 a day at most, each one personal, from our own domain with email authentication set up
(SPF, DKIM and DMARC), or they will land in spam.

## Long walkthrough (6 to 8 minutes, wide, with voice)

| Chapter | Show | Say, in short |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 The problem | Title card | Bookings are handled. Everything else about the property is not |
| 0:30 A property | Overview, codes, amenities | One page per property, seen only by who needs it |
| 1:30 Scheduling | Cleans list, new clean, repeating | Bookings become cleans, regular ones repeat |
| 2:30 The cleaner | Phone, arrive to check out | The same steps every time |
| 4:00 Problems and tasks | Report an issue, make it a task | Nothing sits in a message |
| 4:45 Safety | Dates, reminders, certificates | A starting list, check your local rules |
| 5:30 Stock and linen | Levels, linen screen | A tap, not a count |
| 6:15 Money | Invoices, send to Xero | Hours become invoices. We do not pay anyone |
| 7:00 Join | Register interest | We set early companies up ourselves |

Put chapter times in the video description so people can skip to what they care about.

Use only the made-up demo company on screen (`scripts/marketing/demo-data.ts`). Do not show a real
company, address or code. Do not show alerts arriving until they have been checked on a real phone.
